A wavelength is an address

ObjektiYksi used frequency and ports to interrogate one conserved material. Here the wave's wavenumber selects a spatial standing-wave pattern; a later wave interrogates what remains.

02 / LET STRUCTURE EMERGE

No drawn-in compartments

The substrate starts flat. A driven mode or an incident edge source evolves with the wave equation; a slow thresholded flux moves signed height locally. Different scales share one array, with no interior walls.

03 / TRY TO FALSIFY IT

Control the feedback

Switch off bed-to-wave feedback or reverse event order. Note: a bounded sediment writer can itself be history dependent even when wave feedback is disabled. The comparison also includes a fixed-wave, unbounded control.

Fast field:   u[t+1] = 2u[t] − u[t−1] + Δt² ∂x(c(h)² ∂x u) − damping
Energy:       E(x) = time-average of [∂x u(x,t)]²
Threshold:    M(x) = max(E(x) − θ, 0)
Transport:    q(x+½) = −rate · [M(x+1) − M(x)]
Material:     h(x,t+1) = h(x,t) + q(x−½) − q(x+½)
Address:      wavelength ≈ 2 · domain length / mode number
Research boundary. This is an original interactive 1D numerical instrument, not a faithful replication of G1/G2 held-out benchmarks or the 3112-cell floor in ComputingBeachClaude. The energy-gradient sediment mobility is a toy constitutive law, not calibrated Shields/Exner transport. Material is transported conservatively (up to floating-point roundoff) but threshold, wave amplitudes and display magnification are chosen for exploration. The mode-shaped drive deliberately selects a resonant spatial eigenmode; it does not demonstrate wavelength segregation by an unconstrained incident wave. The incident-edge option is a more difficult control. Writing stress is spatially averaged over a frequency-dependent radius (9, 4, 0 cells), which is an explicit constitutive assumption, not an emergent consequence of water dynamics. The short/long wave imprint relationship is a hypothesis to test across rules, geometries and resolution—not evidence for neural frequency organization.
